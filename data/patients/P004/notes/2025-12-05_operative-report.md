## Procedure
Right hip cemented hemiarthroplasty, posterior approach.

## Anesthesia
Spinal anesthesia with light sedation, chosen to limit airway and lung complications given COPD.

## Findings
Displaced subcapital femoral neck fracture. Good-quality acetabular cartilage.

## Details
Latex-free setup used. Femoral head removed and a cemented stem with a unipolar head placed. Hip stable through full range of motion. Estimated blood loss 300 mL. No intraoperative complications. Posterior hip precautions for 6 weeks. Weight bearing as tolerated.
