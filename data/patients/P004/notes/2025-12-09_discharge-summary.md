## Hospital course
Admitted 2025-12-04 with a displaced right femoral neck fracture. Underwent right hip cemented hemiarthroplasty on 2025-12-05. On post-operative day 2 she had nighttime confusion consistent with delirium, which resolved within 36 hours with sleep hygiene, reorientation, and stopping oxycodone at night. Post-operative hemoglobin fell to 9.8 g/dL; no transfusion was needed and it was 10.3 at discharge. Vitamin D was low at 18 ng/mL. Walked 40 feet with a walker on day 3.

## Medication changes
- Enoxaparin 40 mg subcutaneously daily for 28 days total for blood clot prevention.
- Start vitamin D3 2000 IU daily.
- Stop alendronate because of reflux symptoms and frequently missed weekly doses. Plan yearly IV zoledronic acid after vitamin D is repleted.
- Acetaminophen 1 g every 8 hours as needed; no opioids at discharge.

## Disposition
Discharged to inpatient rehabilitation facility. Posterior hip precautions for 6 weeks. Home safety evaluation before returning home. Follow up with geriatrics and orthopedics in 6-8 weeks.
