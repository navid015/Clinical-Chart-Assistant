## History
78-year-old woman with COPD, osteoporosis on alendronate, and hypertension. Tripped over a rug at home this morning and fell onto her right side; unable to bear weight afterward. No head strike or loss of consciousness. No dizziness or chest pain before the fall. Lives alone in a two-story house.

## Examination
Right leg shortened and externally rotated; pain with any hip movement. Distal pulses and sensation intact. Lungs with mildly decreased breath sounds, no wheeze. SpO2 94% on room air.

## Imaging
Right hip X-ray: displaced femoral neck fracture (Garden IV). Chest X-ray: hyperinflation, no acute findings.

## Assessment and plan
Displaced right femoral neck fracture after a mechanical fall, a fragility fracture in the setting of osteoporosis.
- Plan right hip hemiarthroplasty tomorrow.
- Pain control with acetaminophen and low-dose oxycodone; avoid codeine because of prior nausea and vomiting.
- Latex precautions in the operating room.
- Continue inhaler and amlodipine. Check vitamin D and calcium.
