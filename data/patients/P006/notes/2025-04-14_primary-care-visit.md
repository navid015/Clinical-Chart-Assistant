## Subjective
44-year-old man with Hashimoto hypothyroidism and recurrent depression. Three months of fatigue, low mood, poor concentration and daytime sleepiness. His partner reports loud snoring and pauses in breathing at night. Takes levothyroxine 100 mcg with his morning coffee and breakfast. Taking sertraline 50 mg daily. No known drug allergies.

## Objective
BMI 37.2, BP 134/86, neck circumference 44 cm. Epworth Sleepiness Scale 15. PHQ-9 score 17 (moderately severe). Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale screen negative: denies suicidal thoughts, intent or plan.
Labs: TSH 7.8 mIU/L, free T4 0.8 ng/dL.

## Assessment
1. Hypothyroidism, under-replaced; taking levothyroxine with coffee and food likely reduces absorption.
2. Major depressive disorder, recurrent, moderately severe episode.
3. Suspected obstructive sleep apnea contributing to fatigue and low mood.

## Plan
- Take levothyroxine on an empty stomach 30-60 minutes before breakfast and coffee. Increase dose from 100 mcg to 112 mcg daily. Recheck TSH in 6-8 weeks.
- Increase sertraline from 50 mg to 100 mg daily. Referral to behavioral health for therapy. Crisis line information given.
- Order in-lab polysomnography.
