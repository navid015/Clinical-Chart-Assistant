## Study
In-lab split-night polysomnography.

## Diagnostic portion
Apnea-hypopnea index (AHI) 32 events per hour, mostly obstructive, worse when lying on his back. Oxygen saturation nadir 81%. Time with saturation below 90%: 14% of sleep time.

## CPAP titration
Obstructive events controlled at CPAP 10 cm H2O with a residual AHI of 3 per hour.

## Impression and recommendations
Severe obstructive sleep apnea. Start CPAP at 10 cm H2O nightly with a nasal mask and heated humidification. Avoid driving while drowsy. Weight loss would likely help.
