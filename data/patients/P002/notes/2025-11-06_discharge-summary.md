## Hospital course
Admitted 2025-11-02 for acute on chronic systolic heart failure. Diuresed 5.1 kg with IV furosemide. Atrial fibrillation converted spontaneously to sinus rhythm on hospital day 2. Oxygen weaned to room air on day 3. Echo showed LVEF 30%. Creatinine peaked at 1.4. NT-proBNP fell from 6840 to 3120 pg/mL.

## Discharge condition
Stable. Discharge weight 88.4 kg (dry weight target). NYHA class II-III.

## Medication changes
- Furosemide increased from 20 mg to 40 mg daily.
- Dapagliflozin 10 mg daily started.
- Spironolactone 25 mg daily resumed.
- Continued sacubitril/valsartan 49/51 mg twice daily, metoprolol succinate 100 mg daily, apixaban 5 mg twice daily, atorvastatin 80 mg daily.

## Instructions and follow-up
- 2 g sodium diet and 2 L fluid restriction.
- Weigh daily; call the heart failure clinic for a gain of more than 1 kg in a day or 2 kg in a week.
- Heart failure clinic visit within 7 days; BMP in 1 week.
- Cardiology to consider ICD evaluation if LVEF remains 35% or less after optimized therapy.
