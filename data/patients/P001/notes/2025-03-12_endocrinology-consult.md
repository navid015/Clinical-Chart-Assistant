## Reason for consult
Referred by primary care for type 2 diabetes above goal with declining kidney function.

## History
Completed diabetes education in 2024 and now takes metformin consistently with meals. A1c improved from 8.9% (2024-02) to 7.8% (2025-03-05). Home glucose readings 130-170 mg/dL fasting. No hypoglycemia. Penicillin allergy (hives).

## Data reviewed
eGFR 44 (down from 58 in 2024-02). UACR 110 mg/g. Potassium 4.8. BP today 136/82.

## Assessment
Type 2 diabetes with diabetic kidney disease, now CKD stage 3a (eGFR 44) with albuminuria. A1c 7.8%, above individualized goal of less than 7%.

## Plan
- Reduce metformin from 1000 mg to 500 mg twice daily because eGFR is below 45.
- Start empagliflozin 10 mg once daily for glucose lowering and kidney protection.
- Counseled on genital hygiene and risk of genital yeast infections, and on sick-day rules: hold empagliflozin and metformin during vomiting, poor oral intake, or dehydration.
- Continue lisinopril 20 mg daily.
- Diabetic eye exam is overdue; referral to ophthalmology placed.
- Repeat A1c, eGFR, UACR in 6 months.
