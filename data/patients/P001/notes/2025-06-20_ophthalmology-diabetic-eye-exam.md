## Exam
Visual acuity 20/25 right eye, 20/30 left eye with correction. Dilated fundus exam: scattered microaneurysms and a few dot-blot hemorrhages in both eyes. No neovascularization. No clinically significant macular edema. OCT of the macula normal in both eyes.

## Impression
Mild nonproliferative diabetic retinopathy in both eyes without macular edema.

## Plan
- No treatment needed at this time.
- Continue glucose and blood pressure control.
- Repeat dilated eye exam in 12 months, or sooner for any vision change.
