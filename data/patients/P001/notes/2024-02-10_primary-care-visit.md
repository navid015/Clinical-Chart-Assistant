## Subjective
56-year-old woman with type 2 diabetes since 2014, hypertension and hyperlipidemia, here for routine follow-up. Reports missing evening metformin doses about three times a week because of stomach upset when taken without food. No polyuria, chest pain, or foot wounds. Walks 15 minutes most days.

## Objective
BP 148/90, HR 78, BMI 33.1. Monofilament sensation intact both feet. No foot ulcers.
Labs today: Hemoglobin A1c 8.9%. eGFR 58. Urine albumin/creatinine ratio 45 mg/g. LDL 128 mg/dL. Potassium 4.4.

## Assessment
1. Type 2 diabetes, above goal (A1c 8.9%), partly related to missed doses.
2. Hypertension, above goal.
3. Moderately increased albuminuria (UACR 45 mg/g) with eGFR 58.
4. Hyperlipidemia, LDL above goal.

## Plan
- Continue metformin 1000 mg twice daily; counseled to take with meals to reduce GI upset.
- Increase lisinopril from 10 mg to 20 mg daily for blood pressure and albuminuria.
- Increase atorvastatin from 20 mg to 40 mg daily.
- Referral to diabetes education program.
- Repeat A1c, BMP and UACR in 6 months.
