## Subjective
Asthma much better since starting maintenance and reliever therapy: no night waking and reliever use about twice a week. Asthma Control Test score 22. Main concern today is migraines, now 4-5 per month, each lasting up to a day. Sumatriptan helps most attacks. Uses a levonorgestrel IUD placed in 2023; not planning pregnancy.

## Assessment
1. Moderate persistent asthma, now well controlled.
2. Episodic migraine without aura with increasing frequency; preventive therapy indicated.

## Plan
- Migraine prevention: propranolol avoided because nonselective beta-blockers can worsen bronchospasm in asthma. Start topiramate 25 mg nightly, increase to 50 mg nightly after 2 weeks.
- Counseled on topiramate side effects (tingling, word-finding difficulty, kidney stones) and that it can harm a developing fetus; she has reliable contraception with her IUD.
- Headache diary; limit sumatriptan to no more than 9 days per month to avoid medication-overuse headache.
- Continue budesonide/formoterol regimen and cetirizine.
- Follow up in 8 weeks.
