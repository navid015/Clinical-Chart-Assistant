## Reason for consult
Asthma exacerbation requiring ED care and oral steroids in October 2025.

## Spirometry
FEV1 2.21 L (72% of predicted), FEV1/FVC 0.68. After bronchodilator, FEV1 rose by 350 mL (14%), a significant bronchodilator response consistent with asthma.

## Other data
Blood eosinophils 420 cells/uL. Total IgE 210 IU/mL. Asthma Control Test score 14 (not well controlled). Lives with a cat.

## Assessment
Moderate persistent asthma, not well controlled, eosinophilic phenotype with allergic sensitization to cat dander. Inhaler technique was suboptimal and was corrected today.

## Plan
- Stop fluticasone and albuterol. Start budesonide/formoterol 160/4.5 mcg, 2 puffs twice daily plus 1 puff as needed for symptoms (maintenance and reliever therapy).
- Written asthma action plan provided.
- Keep the cat out of the bedroom; HEPA filter recommended.
- If still uncontrolled after 3 months with good adherence, consider biologic therapy given eosinophils above 300.
