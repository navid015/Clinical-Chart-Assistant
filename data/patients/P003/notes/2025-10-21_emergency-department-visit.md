## History
33-year-old woman with asthma since childhood, on fluticasone 110 mcg 2 puffs twice daily and albuterol as needed. Five days of cough and runny nose followed by 2 days of worsening wheeze and chest tightness; used albuterol every 2-3 hours for the past day. Admits to missing fluticasone doses. No prior intubations or ICU admissions. One previous ED visit for asthma in 2021. No known drug allergies.

## Examination
HR 112, RR 26, SpO2 93% on room air, afebrile. Speaking in full sentences with diffuse expiratory wheeze. Peak expiratory flow 210 L/min (55% of predicted).

## Treatment
Three nebulized albuterol/ipratropium treatments and oral prednisone 40 mg. After treatment: peak flow 360 L/min (about 85% of predicted), SpO2 97%, minimal wheeze. Eosinophils 380 cells/uL.

## Disposition
Moderate asthma exacerbation triggered by a viral upper respiratory infection, improved in the ED. Discharged home on prednisone 40 mg daily for 5 days total. Counseled on daily controller adherence and inhaler technique. Follow up with primary care or pulmonology within 1-2 weeks.
