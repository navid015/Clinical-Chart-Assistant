## History
51-year-old man with stage IIIB (pT3 N1a M0) sigmoid adenocarcinoma, pMMR, resected on 2025-08-18 with negative margins. Recovered well from surgery. ECOG performance status 0. Pre-operative CEA 6.8 ng/mL; post-operative CEA 2.1 ng/mL today.

## Pharmacogenomics
DPYD genotyping: no variants detected (normal DPD metabolizer). Standard fluoropyrimidine dosing is appropriate.

## Assessment
Low-risk stage III colon cancer (T1-3 and N1). Adjuvant chemotherapy reduces recurrence risk.

## Plan
- Adjuvant CAPOX for 3 months (4 cycles every 21 days): oxaliplatin 130 mg/m2 IV on day 1 and capecitabine 1000 mg/m2 by mouth twice daily on days 1-14.
- Discussed side effects: cold-triggered and cumulative neuropathy from oxaliplatin, hand-foot syndrome and diarrhea from capecitabine, nausea, low blood counts.
- Start cycle 1 on 2025-09-22.
