## Indication
Iron deficiency anemia.

## Findings
Upper endoscopy: mild erosive esophagitis (LA grade A). No ulcers or bleeding source.
Colonoscopy: excellent bowel preparation, cecum reached. A 3.5 cm friable, partially obstructing mass in the sigmoid colon at 25 cm from the anal verge; multiple biopsies taken and the site tattooed. Two sessile polyps of 4 and 6 mm in the ascending colon removed with cold snare.

## Impression
Sigmoid colon mass concerning for malignancy, likely the cause of the anemia. Pathology pending.
