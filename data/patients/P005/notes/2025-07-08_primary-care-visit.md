## Subjective
51-year-old man with GERD reporting 3 months of fatigue and shortness of breath climbing stairs. No black stools noticed, no weight loss. Has never had colorectal cancer screening. Father had colon polyps; no known family history of colon cancer.

## Objective
HR 92, BP 124/78. Pale conjunctivae. Abdomen soft, non-tender.
Labs: hemoglobin 10.1 g/dL, MCV 74 fL, ferritin 8 ng/mL.

## Assessment
Iron deficiency anemia in an adult man, which requires evaluation for gastrointestinal blood loss. Screening colonoscopy is also overdue.

## Plan
- Urgent referral to gastroenterology for colonoscopy and upper endoscopy.
- Start ferrous sulfate 325 mg daily.
- Recheck hemoglobin after endoscopic evaluation.
