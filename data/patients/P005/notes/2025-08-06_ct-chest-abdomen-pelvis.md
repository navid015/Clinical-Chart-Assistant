## Technique
CT of the chest, abdomen and pelvis with IV contrast. Patient premedicated for prior contrast reaction (hives) with prednisone 50 mg at 13, 7 and 1 hour and diphenhydramine 50 mg 1 hour before the scan. No reaction occurred.

## Findings
Circumferential wall thickening of the sigmoid colon over 4 cm, corresponding to the known mass. A few pericolic lymph nodes, the largest 8 mm. Liver without lesions. Lungs clear, no nodules. No ascites or peritoneal disease.

## Impression
Sigmoid colon cancer with possible regional nodal involvement. No evidence of distant metastases.
